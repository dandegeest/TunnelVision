WARDROBE A→B · SEPTEMBER 5, 2026

Camotion endpoints and Cinematographer locomotion language appear complementary.

TunnelVision is agentic filmmaking research extending filmmaker Terran Boylan's original TunnelVision technique. This chapter records a controlled Seedance 2.5 ablation on the Integration Test 01 A→B pair. It is not Camotion 01.13. The 01.8 operator was not changed. No images or Camotion frames were regenerated.

Notation: A / B = pristine canonicals. A′ / B′ = Camotion-conditioned shooting frames. A→B = generated traversal shot.

Camotion is motion-state conditioning, not image enhancement.

01.12 remains true on Ghost Library stills: there was no useful pristine-source operating window where substantial baked travel could be encoded without objectionable structured copies. Today's video evidence is complementary, not contradictory. A′ does not need to be an aesthetically superior still. It may function as machine-facing motion-state conditioning for the video model.

Independent human review: strongest spatial and temporal continuity of the four. Neither pristine+locomotion nor conditioned+minimal matched the combination. The wardrobe/clothes → snowy forest passage was judged even better than the original unseeded Integration Test 01 A→B. Bedroom → wardrobe threshold → hanging clothes passing the camera → dark intermediate volume → snow. No intermediate canonical was supplied.

Retain Camotion + Cinematographer prompt design in Phase 1.

This is one controlled seed, not statistical proof, not a claim that Camotion is universally necessary, and not a claim that the current operator is optimal. It is enough evidence to keep both complementary pieces in the working pipeline: the CM prompt supplies semantic/physical locomotion intent; Camotion supplies visual motion-state conditioning; Seedance combines them.

Spatial continuity is not temporal continuity

EASING

Forward motion is not the same as velocity continuing through B. Seedance often appears to ease/resolve toward the endpoint, so B can behave like a stop rather than a sampled position on a continuing trajectory. Canonical frames should be position samples, not stop points. 04 was perceived as better at retaining ongoing motion at B.

A crude exploratory frame-to-frame visual-change check performed outside the repo suggested increasing final-half-second activity 01≈0.96, 02≈3.47, 03≈4.32, 04≈5.33. Those numbers are not camera velocity. They can include snow, morphing, and exposure change. Informal observation only.

Do not mechanically insert intermediate canonicals

FUTURE CM

04 did not receive an extra canonical X. The video model invented useful intermediate wardrobe volume from conditioned endpoints, geometry, and detailed route language. Future CM reasoning: can this pair be covered as one continuous spatial move? If yes, describe/condition the route. If no — as in the Integration Test E→A flat door — recommend an intermediate canonical. Not implemented here.
