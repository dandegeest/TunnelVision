Live Director experiment

TWO INDEPENDENT RUNS

On September 6, 2026, two independent live Director calls were made with the same inputs:

Wardrobe Loop filmmaker story + authoritative starting frame A → Director → Gemini 3.1 Pro via Replicate → validated DirectorPlan.

Neither call used a mock or fixture fallback. Starting frame A was not regenerated.

First live plan

CAPTURED A→G

First captured plan, in travel order:

  • A — attic bedroom / approach wardrobe
  • B — push through hanging coats; wardrobe opens into winter landscape
  • C — emerge into snowy forest; path leads toward ice cavern
  • D — traverse ice cavern; stone doorway visible at far end
  • E — pass through doorway; ascend floating staircase toward warm doorway
  • F — enter observatory; original attic visible through giant lens/window
  • G — cross lens/window threshold back into original attic

Second live plan

DIFFERENT GEOGRAPHY

A second independent live run produced different geography:

wardrobe → snowy path → cave mouth → root cave/tunnel → stone bridge → floating door → bedroom

Initial observation, not a validated general conclusion.

Both live samples organized the journey around traversable thresholds and intermediate spatial geography rather than merely producing a sequence of disconnected visual scenes. Examples included hanging coats / wardrobe threshold; a path leading to a cave mouth; a tunnel leading to a doorway; a staircase leading to a doorway; a bridge leading to a floating door; and a destination world becoming visible through a threshold before crossing it.

This is encouraging evidence that the Director prompt is expressing the existing TunnelVision research principle that visual continuity alone is insufficient and that the camera needs a spatially intelligible route.

Director semantic/spatial planning success ≠ validated physical shootability.

Do not claim that these routes are actually shootable yet. The Cinematographer has not inspected generated sets because destination imagery has not been generated. A plausible planned route is not a shootable pair of sets.
