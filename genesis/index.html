TunnelVision

Origins / Attribution

TunnelVision originated with filmmaker Terran Boylan, who developed the concept and manual filmmaking technique through work for his own YouTube channel and video content. The technique was later adapted for the Digging in the Dirt project, where we used the playful name TunnelTV. The research documented here extends Terran's original TunnelVision work into an agentic filmmaking system.

Original TunnelVision concept and manual technique — Terran Boylan
Agentic Director/Cinematographer architecture and Camotion research — Dan DeGeest

Key findings

  • Visual / world continuity ≠ spatial traversability ≠ continuous camera travel
  • Continuous locomotion prompting outranks destination matching
  • Depth weighting improved motion conditioning on Ghost Library
  • Our analysis of Terran's original TunnelVision Photoshop workflow indicates depth-banded exposure compositing rather than a single continuously depth-scaled blur
  • Motion-treating the strong depth mask was important in 01.5
  • Terminal-at-canonical start exposure worsened Seedance behavior through Krea (01.6). A later Replicate rerun did not reproduce that severe collapse/retreat
  • Halving strength removed obvious recursion but weakened geometry authority (01.7)
  • 01.8 preserved more central route geometry at still level. Krea later opened backward, then went forward; a Replicate rerun did not reproduce that pronounced reverse-then-forward opening. Those Krea behaviors should not currently be treated as intrinsic to the Camotion variants. 01.8 Route-Preserved Exposure remains the current Camotion baseline, not a promotion over 01.5 as CameraMotionPlan
  • Environmental and foreground motion can act as a perceptual witness to camera locomotion. Observation/hypothesis, not a proven mechanism
  • On one Seedance prompt-control pair, slowing the locomotion prompt produced a slower traversal; adding walking-embodiment language did not clearly produce footstep-driven motion. One-run observations, not proof
  • 01.9 densified 01.8 exposure taps to ≤1 px spacing (2–98 samples). The repeated-object appearance did not clearly go away (MAE vs 01.8 ≈ 0.25, max channel difference 7). Sparse sampling is unlikely to be the dominant cause on this fixture. 01.9 is not promoted. No 01.9 video test
  • 01.10 extracted real 01.8 compositor intermediates. Discrete ghost copies are already visible in strong exposure. The multi-layer compositor hypothesis is not supported as the origin of first appearance on this fixture. 01.10 is not promoted. No 01.10 video test
  • 01.11 characterized the exposure operator on a synthetic fixture and Ghost Library artifact crops. 01.8 fixed-16 is discrete; 01.9 dense sampling fills the local point streak; structured features still read as transformed copies. Sparse sampling is not the dominant remaining cause. No 01.11 candidate is promoted. No 01.11 video test
  • 01.12 tested 01.8 fixed-16 gather at strengths 0.02 / 0.04 / 0.06 / 0.08, pristine vs sigma=1 diagnostic control. No useful pristine operating window: the motion cue becomes useful at about the same point structured duplication becomes objectionable (classification C + D). Exposure-operator tuning is closed. No 01.12 condition is promoted. No 01.12 video test
  • Directed A→B requires endpoint fidelity. Canonical spatial authority, Camotion motion conditioning, and generative-video improvisation remain distinct
  • Integration Test 01 produced a coherent unattended five-shot loop. A destination object is not enough; the shot needs traversable depth through the transition. Movie-level camera-speed jumps at shot boundaries are currently accidental
  • Do not abandon Camotion. Compelling traversal shots occurred with pinned 01.8. The movie-level metric is whether the model shoots the route, not whether A′ is aesthetically clean as a still
  • On one controlled Wardrobe A→B Seedance seed (70), Camotion-conditioned endpoints plus the authoritative Cinematographer locomotion prompt were complementary and stronger than either factor alone. Camotion remains motion-state conditioning, not image enhancement. 01.12’s still-image structured-copy limit is not contradicted
  • On three already-shootable Wardrobe Loop pairs, scene-aware bounded Camotion strength {.02,.04,.08} was preferred over fixed .08 (human 3–0, independent second 3–0). Strength is a per-canonical Cinematographer choice. Camotion Phase 1 is frozen
  • On Wardrobe E→A, the Cinematographer independently judged the direct route NEEDS_INTERMEDIATE, proposed one spatial camera position X, inspected the actual generated X, and rejected both E→X and X→A. Protocol stopped before video. Generated sets are evidence, not assumed success. Research Phase 1 is complete
  • Product Slice 1 is the first product UI: Plan states the intended journey; Shoot turns Destinations into positions and Journey into camera travel. Shootability is relational on the route. Fixture-driven; generation is not connected
  • Product Slice 2 makes Plan a conversational storyboard: intent enters through Conversation; the storyboard is the planning artifact. Shoot remains production reality. Visual checkpoint: 11 — Product Slice 2
  • Product Slice 3 is the first autonomous Director step. Two live Gemini 3.1 Pro plans from the Wardrobe Loop story plus starting frame A organized journeys around traversable thresholds. That is semantic/spatial planning observation, not validated physical shootability. Destination imagery was not generated in that slice. Later work added filmmaker story editing, starting-frame upload, and sequential Derived construction. Observation: 12 — Product Slice 3
  • A later fixture-independent Director plan from an uploaded alien salt-flat A grounded on the image's monolith/fissure rather than Wardrobe caption text. First image-conditioned destination construction (FLUX Kontext Pro) strongly preserved world/material continuity and produced substantial forward camera displacement, but did not fully cross the Director-specified threshold. Sequential Derived construction later produced the forest A–F canonicals. Local continuity can remain strong while large visual transformation accumulates, and generated reality can diverge from the original open-loop Director plan. Constructed stills are not shootable evidence. Observation: 13 — Destination construction
  • A forest A→F evidence spike on those actual constructed destinations produced a directionally coherent 30-second Camotion + Seedance journey (forest → mouth → tube → crystal → portal → void) without changing Director, Camotion, or product architecture. Destination/world continuity did not automatically produce physical traversal continuity. A→B: convincing forward locomotion. D→E: useful threshold grammar (next space visible through the remaining opening before the previous space disappeared). C→D: early endpoint attraction / weak start authority. E→F: open-void geometry plus radial-forward Camotion read as warp rather than traversal. Human review of the assembled movie was positive. Not a Render Movie product feature. Do not conclude that Camotion failed. Observation: 14 — Forest A→F

The production ladder is to block / blocked / shooting / complete. Visual checkpoint: 15 — Product Slice 4
