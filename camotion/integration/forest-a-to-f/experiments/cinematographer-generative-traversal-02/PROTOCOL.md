# Forest Cinematographer Experiment 02: Generative Traversal Potential

Research / evidence. Not a product PR. Does not overwrite Experiment 01.

## Hypothesis

Experiment 01 asked whether two stills can be proven as physically adjacent
sets with a directly visible route. TunnelVision may instead need to know
whether a generative video model can plausibly synthesize continuous
forward camera travel from visual state A to visual state B.

## Single variable

Experiment-only Cinematographer prompt. Same model, images, project
context, output schema, and pairs as Experiment 01. The evaluation
objective is generative traversal potential, not literal geometric proof
of adjacency.

Pairs: A→B, C→D, D→E, E→F.

Do not supply prior video outcomes or expected classifications.
